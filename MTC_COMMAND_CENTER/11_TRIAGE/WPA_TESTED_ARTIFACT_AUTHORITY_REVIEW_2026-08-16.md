NEEDS-REWORK

## Bottom line

The design does not close the gap it was created to close. Its local record grammar, no-replace write, zero/one/many checks, and A-versus-B comparison are well specified, but the authority is still not demonstrably independent of the later freezer. The gap's first requirement is that the freezer cannot influence the expected identity (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/MISSING_ARTIFACT_WPA_IDENTITY_AUTHORITY_2026-08-16.md:42-49`). The freeze procedure makes that concrete: a distinct WP-A Capture Authority, a registry published by someone other than the freezer, proof that the freezer had no post-close mutation path, and direct auditor acquisition (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:66-77`). The proposed design supplies none of those mechanisms and expressly leaves principal separation or an independently controlled ledger/signature `UNKNOWN` (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:150-152`).

## Required findings

### 1. Independence is asserted through Git chronology, not established

The producer is named as the “WP-A close operation,” and the authority plus four captures are placed in a commit with trailers (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:70-89`). A commit trailer, an add-only tree transition, and ancestry prove what bytes occur in a history; they do not prove who observed the tested artifact, who created the commit, or that the freezer could not run the producer or write equivalent bytes. The design itself correctly says the JSON `producer` field is only an assertion (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:89`). The actual producer principal/process, its separation from the freezer, the freezer's write capability after close, and any independently controlled publication anchor are all `UNKNOWN`.

That is fatal rather than a residual threat-model nicety. The freeze procedure requires real evidence that the freezer has no mutation path and says hashes cannot manufacture that independence (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:70-77`). It also prohibits R23 publication when actual independence is not established or when the freezer can mutate an authority source (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:857-868`). The design narrows its guarantee to a “non-destructive Git workflow” (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:150-152`), but the task's required boundary is between actors, not merely between historical blobs.

The selector retains another influence path. It starts from the freezer's proposed commit `F` and a run ID `R`; the independent source that preregisters `R` is explicitly `UNKNOWN` (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:154-174`, `MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:261-268`). Although multiple matching close commits in one selected ancestry stop, nothing independently pins which close commit/history and run ID the freezer must consume. Whether the freezer can rerun the producer under another run ID or choose a branch containing a preferred close is therefore `UNKNOWN`. The required design must make those choices unavailable, not merely forbid them in prose.

Required repair: appoint a concrete WP-A Capture Authority distinct from the freezer; define the two principals/process identities and capabilities; publish the closed authority into storage the freezer cannot create, replace, delete, or republish; have a separately controlled pre-R23 registry pin the run ID, close/publication identity, descriptor, and exact bytes; and require auditors to acquire that registry and publication independently. If no such enforcement is available, F5 must remain BLOCKED.

### 2. The proposed authority is not consumable by the stated freeze procedure

The freeze procedure requires a detached descriptor and manifest, an enumerated member root, exactly one producer-separation member, exactly one events member, and exactly one tested-artifact member (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:79-118`). Its F5 selector expects two tested-artifact rows—one `GIT_OBJECT` and one `WORKTREE_RAW`—with `capture_id`, `artifact_id`, `repo_relative_path`, `tested_sha`, mode-specific blob/byte/SHA fields, and a single shared path (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:706-768`).

The design instead defines one repository JSONL row whose identity is an installed-release tuple `(git_object_format, tested_release_oid, release-manifest bytes, release-manifest SHA-256)` and four captured files (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:8-17`, `MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:43-62`, `MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:95-114`). It has no freeze-v3 descriptor, external manifest, producer-separation record, `WPA_COMPLETED` event row, capture/artifact IDs, artifact path, or paired Git/raw artifact identities. Freeze v3 cannot execute §8.3-§8.6 against this schema. The design says freeze v3 must later be amended, but does not provide a field-for-field replacement procedure (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:271-283`).

Required repair: choose one canonical contract. Either implement the exact freeze-v3 publication and selector shape, or replace freeze v3's conditional code with a complete, literal verifier for the JSONL tuple and explain precisely how installed-payload identity proves continuity to the final artifact that freeze v3 currently compares in Git-object and raw-file modes. No adapter written or selected by the freezer may become the trust bridge.

### 3. Create-once is strong inside the proposed tool but not at the authority boundary

The proposed `close` operation has good local semantics: exclusive installation, directory fsync, refusal on an existing final path, and no overwrite/repair path (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:118-131`). The second invocation has an explicit STOP result and leaves the original file untouched. The descendant-lineage check also detects changes to the chosen authority blob (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:133-150`).

Those properties bind only callers that use this tool and the selected first-parent history. Enforcement preventing a direct writer, a fresh worktree/clone, a different branch, or a replacement publication is `UNKNOWN`. “The final-freeze tool has no create option” is an interface restriction, not proof that the freezing party has no other write route (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:76-77`). Freeze v3 separately records the real create-once/read-only mechanism and namespace as `UNKNOWN` and requires evidence that the freezer has no write path (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:46-50`).

Required repair: specify and test the storage-level exclusion, including direct-write, delete/recreate, alternate-publication, alternate-branch, and rerun attacks. The verifier must reject zero or multiple independently published close identities, not just zero or multiple rows in whichever history `F` happens to select.

## Axis assessment

### Selector

The row-level and close-commit cardinality rules are sound in isolation. They strict-parse the whole file, reject zero matches, reject multiple matches, and forbid first/latest/preferred selection (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:154-174`). This satisfies the narrow selector requirement. It does not cure the unanchored choice of `R`, `F`, or publication described above. Freeze v3 instead makes an independently published registry the sole selection authority (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:451-461`).

### Recomputability

An auditor could recompute the record's arithmetic if all of the following are retained and independently anchored: the WP-A close commit; the authority blob and its trailers/tree entry; all four captured blobs; the exact schema and RFC-8785 implementation; the pinned producer/verifier bytes; the Git object format and complete object database for `tested_release_oid`; the exact release-manifest member inventory, ordering, `RELEASE_SHA` rendering, separator rendering, and refusal rules; and the proposed final artifact in the identity mode being compared. The design describes most computation steps (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:201-222`), while the cited derivation defines Git-object membership, order, separator variants, and refusal conditions (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPL_P2_STAGING_WPLP2-20260809T125940Z-8dc78f08/02_PREREG/CANDIDATE_RELEASE_DERIVATION.md:47-61`, `MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPL_P2_STAGING_WPLP2-20260809T125940Z-8dc78f08/02_PREREG/CANDIDATE_RELEASE_DERIVATION.md:76-119`).

Retention of the complete object database and independent acquisition anchors is `UNKNOWN`. More importantly, recomputing hashes from captured files proves those bytes' consistency, not that the freezer-independent WP-A actor captured them from the artifact actually tested. Freeze v3 explicitly distinguishes arithmetic, binding, and real producer truth (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/AUDIT2_READINESS_PACKAGE/FREEZE_PROCEDURES_V3_2026-08-16.md:838-845`). Recomputability is therefore technically plausible but authority provenance remains unproved.

### Failure demonstration

The predicate is not inherently self-confirming. If a fixed authority genuinely publishes Artifact A and the freezer later proposes a fully evaluable Artifact B, the differing release OIDs make the intended mismatch reachable (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:224-244`). The matrix also names GREEN, zero-match, multiple-match, second-create, and corruption arms and correctly says no implementation evidence exists yet (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:246-257`).

The named B fixture is not yet a complete executable RED: its manifest SHA is `UNKNOWN`, no retained fully evaluable B artifact is established, and the verifier is not implemented (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:228-244`, `MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:246-257`). Under the design's own exit table, missing objects or incomplete traversal must STOP rather than produce the desired mismatch FAIL (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:188-199`). Closure therefore needs two complete valid artifacts, the unchanged independently anchored A authority, and literal RED-then-GREEN runs through the production verifier. It also needs attack fixtures showing that the freezer cannot republish B as the expected authority.

### Sequencing

The temporal placement is mostly actionable: pin the mechanism before WP-A, observe immediately before the first test and after the last, create the row during WP-A close, commit before host discard, and make the later freezer read-only (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:70-89`). It also places publication inside existing step 5 and lists the major documents that must be amended before dispatch (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:271-284`). Producing the run-specific observation at WP-A close, rather than guessing it before the run, is correct (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:19`).

The missing steps are the decisive ones: appointment and preflight of the independent Capture Authority, publication under a proven freezer-inaccessible mechanism, independent preregistration of `R`, and publication of the pre-R23 registry that pins the unique close/publication. Their owners, concrete storage mechanism, and enforcement evidence are `UNKNOWN`. The WP-A closure template is also still unnamed (`MTC_COMMAND_CENTER/11_TRIAGE/WPA_TESTED_ARTIFACT_AUTHORITY_DESIGN_2026-08-16.md:275-284`). Until those steps and the freeze-v3 schema alignment are added, WP-A must not start and F5 must remain open.

## Acceptance condition

A repaired design can be reconsidered only after it proves the freezer lacks every producer, mutation, rerun, and selection path; defines one schema that the production freeze checker actually consumes; pins the run/publication through an independent registry; preserves all recomputation inputs; and records production-checker RED/GREEN plus create-once, zero/multiple, direct-write, rerun, and alternate-history attacks. This review grants no acceptance, authorization, or permission for any operational action.
