## 1A. Reproduced boundary difference — duplicate ingest (executed evidence, E2)

`archive.append_bar` on the identical first bar returns `IDENTICAL_REPLAY_NOOP` (dedupe at
`market_data_collector.py:311-314`). But the same raw `WS_LIVE` bar fed twice through
`MarketDataCollector.ingest` refuses before the archive is ever consulted: the second call
reaches `gap_detector(previous, bar.bar_open_time, ...)` with `previous == bar_open_time`
→ `detect_gap` sees `distance = 0` → raises `CollectionRefused("bar sequence is
non-increasing or off interval")` (`:255-258`, called from `:364-369`). Persisted rows stay
at 1 either way (E2), so the durable fence holds; the divergence is purely in the public
ingest disposition: NOOP-at-archive vs REFUSED-at-collector.

This corrects any blanket "the collector is idempotent for identical replays" claim: only
the archive boundary is idempotent; the collector boundary refuses duplicates. Exact
reproduction inputs and observed/expected dispositions are recorded in E2 — not a collector
failure and not an acceptance result.

Proposed (NOT implemented, requires separate owner authorization as its own slice):
a bounded duplicate-ingest fix that performs **read-only duplicate classification
before gap validation**. It must **not** append the incoming bar before the gap check.
A first-seen incoming bar is **not persisted** if gap-fill refuses. Preserve: conflict
refusal (C4), gap completeness (C6), no-network (I3), and the forming-bar fence (C5).
A discriminating fixture is T7 in §4. Implementation is out of scope for this lane.
