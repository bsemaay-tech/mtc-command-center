## Review cadence

- Review on every safety-critical task, incident, dependency change, monitoring-window reset, deployment change, or ADR status change.
- A High-impact risk without an active prevention/containment owner blocks Gate D or higher.
- Risk acceptance for financial, secret, live, or migration risks belongs to Barış, not an AI agent.
