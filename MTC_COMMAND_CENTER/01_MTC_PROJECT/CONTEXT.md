# MTC terminology

- **MTC:** Master Trading Core.
- **Producer parity:** raw signal agreement for one standalone producer.
- **Controller integration:** gated composition into `MTC_V2.pine`; distinct from producer parity.
- **PineTS/Python PASS:** agreement between those implementations only; not automatically a fresh
  TradingView PASS.
- **MTC-Engine Validation:** shortlist-only intermediate funnel reusing `MTCRunner`.
