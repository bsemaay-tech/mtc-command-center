## E-5 — B-15 joint-ratification request package (addressed to the P013 build owner and the WP-P0-08 migration owner; not sent tonight)

### E-5.1 The question, quoted from the design (`DSGN:1706-1727`, blocker B-15 `RUN-CELL-COORDINATE-CHANNEL`, opened in v1.8)
> "Through which declared member of the closed `CompletedRunInput` do the completed run's `strategy`, `symbol`, and `timeframe` cell coordinates reach `RunCellDimensionsFactory`?"

> "Required resolution: the P0-13 build owner, jointly with the WP-P0-08 migration owner for the emitter that will be the sole direct caller, ratifies the declared member of `CompletedRunInput` that carries the cell coordinates — its name, its type, and the rule that the factory may read no other source — before `RunCellDimensionsFactory` may emit a receipt, before the `strategy=`, `symbol=`, and `timeframe=` Hive segments may be derived, and before F-13 or R-8 may be constructed. This design lane will not add the member on its own authority: assembling an input list is the act that produced this finding. This blocker is **not** P0-20-gated."

### E-5.2 Seam facts attached (V5 §3.3, read against the P020 worktree at the time of V5; re-verify line numbers before sending)
- The per-cell dict returned by the driver's worker already carries `strategy`, `symbol`, `timeframe` (and `classification`) as **emitted values**, not path segments — "precisely the property B-15 demands" (V5 §3.3). This is the natural source of the member's value.
- The per-trial channel does **not** exist yet: the per-parameter `configs` list is built and discarded; only pool aggregates and `summary.best_params` survive (V5 §3.3) — M-3 is a separate owed item and is not part of this ratification.
- The finalization hook (M-2) does not exist; B-15 concerns the **member**, not the hook.

### E-5.3 The ratification asked for (three parts; the request states them as options so the owners decide, the Lead does not)
| Part | Options | Note |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Member **name** | `run_cell_coordinates` (recommended, mirrors `RunCellDimensionsFactory`) / `cell_coordinates` / other | must not collide with the six declared members or any reserved key |
| (b) Member **type** | a closed frozen value type `RunCellCoordinatesV1(strategy: NonEmptyStr, symbol: NonEmptyStr, timeframe: NonEmptyStr)` with `extra="forbid"` (recommended) / three separate scalar members | one member keeps the "single channel" rule checkable |
| (c) The **sole-source rule** | `RunCellDimensionsFactory` reads the member and nothing else — no Hive path segment, no envelope field, no caller argument; a receipt whose Hive segments disagree with the member is refused (recommended, verbatim from the blocker's intent) | the D026 fixture: a run with member `strategy=A` and a caller-supplied path `strategy=B` must be refused RED-without/GREEN-with |
Also to record: B-15 does **not** re-open B-14 (the factory's authority) nor B-03/D-03 (whether the three become `TrialRecord` columns).
