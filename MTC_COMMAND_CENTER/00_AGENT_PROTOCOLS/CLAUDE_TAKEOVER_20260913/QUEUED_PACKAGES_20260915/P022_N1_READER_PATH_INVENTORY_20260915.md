## 1. What a "research record" is, for this inventory
A shadow/research record = any artifact from which a reader can learn a strategy's performance over a window: backtest/optimisation outputs (`top_results.json`, per-run result folders under the backtest output roots), the research registries (`05_REGISTRY/*.json`, `STRATEGY_RESEARCH_REGISTRY.json` built by `03_QUANTLENS/tools/build_strategy_research_registry.py`), night artifacts (`night_artifacts_reader.py` scans a results root + its most recent subdirectories), the P0-22 tracker's own SQLite (register/disclose/status), reports under `04_REPORTS`, and every copy of these (git history, CT13 records, chat transcripts, AI-session scratch).
