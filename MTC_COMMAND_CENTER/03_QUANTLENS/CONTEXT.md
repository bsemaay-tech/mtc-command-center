# QuantLens terminology

- **QuantLens:** research engine/lab plus AI expert-verdict labels; it does not own numeric truth.
- **Scorecard:** canonical numeric assessment layer.
- **research_robust:** Stage-1 research filter; never inherently promotable.
- **robust_final:** stricter promotion-facing label requiring the full canonical evidence chain.
- **DSR:** deflated Sharpe ratio; multiple-testing adjustment, not a substitute for other gates.
- **BH-FDR:** Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery control across tested hypotheses.
- **CPCV:** combinatorial purged cross-validation.
- **PBO:** probability of backtest overfitting.
- **SYSTEM_TEST_ONLY:** plumbing evidence only—not strategy, paper/live, promotion, or profitability
  evidence.
